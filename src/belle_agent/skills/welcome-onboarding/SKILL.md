# Welcome onboarding

Apply this Skill only when a person has just joined the team. On any other
turn, ignore it.

## Gather what the team has recorded

Read what you can reach in this environment: your memory (start from its index,
then any notes on the team, its project, its people, or its communication
spaces), and any team documents, channel descriptions, or pinned items you have
access to. Teams record different things, so do not expect every topic below.

## Choose the content

Build the message from the topics you found information for. Possible topics:

- the team, its purpose, and its current project or product
- the people to know: leads, an assigned buddy or mentor, who to ask for what
- the channels or spaces, and what each one is for
- recurring meetings, rituals, and upcoming events
- working norms: hours, time zones, response expectations, language
- where documentation lives: handbook, wiki, runbooks, decision records
- tools and access: accounts to request and how to request them
- engineering setup: repositories, development environment, review process
- suggested first steps or first tasks
- required trainings, policies, or compliance steps
- a glossary of the team's acronyms and internal names

If the join event or your memory says something about the new member, such as
their name, role, or sub-team, greet them by name and prefer the topics that
matter for that role.

Leave out a topic when you have no information for it. Do not mention that it
is missing, and do not fill it with general advice or guesses. Always include a
short introduction to yourself and what you can help with, so the message is
useful even when the team has recorded little else.

Share only what any team member may see. Leave out credentials, personal notes
about people, and anything recorded as private.

## Write the message

Keep it short enough to read in a minute or two. Link to documents rather than
copying them. Render channels, people, and documents as the platform's native
links or mentions, not as plain text or Markdown.

Do not ask for more details. Write the message from what you found.

## Deliver it

Send the message privately to the person who joined. Do not post it in the
channel or space they joined, and do not send it to anyone else.
